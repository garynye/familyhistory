# Rights and provenance

The MIT license in this repository applies only to newly written site code,
styles, scripts, and preservation tooling.

Historical photographs, scanned records, genealogical data, transcriptions,
and other archived family material retain their existing copyright and other
applicable rights. Their presence in this public preservation repository does
not place them in the public domain or grant a blanket reuse license.

Each generated page identifies its originating Homestead collection. The
machine-readable `archive/manifest.json` records source URLs, capture dates,
file sizes, content types, and SHA-256 checksums.

Questions about attribution, corrections, or removal may be raised through the
repository's GitHub issue tracker.
