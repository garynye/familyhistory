Denmark
I have been to Denmark several times and travelled quite a bit throughout the country.  There is still much to see, but this page has a few of my favorite  pictures.
In May 2008, I stayed with friends, Arne & Bodil Nielsen at their house in Ballerup.  They took me to some very interesting places.
We drove all around the countryside outside of Copenhagen and saw a lot of lovely scenery.
We went to Fredericksburg Castle.  It is lovely and in a beautiful park-like setting.
