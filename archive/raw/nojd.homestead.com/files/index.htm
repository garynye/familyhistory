The Ancestry of Ellen Augusta Nye

Welcome to my web site. The purpose of the web site is to share my research into the ancestry of my grandmother, Ellen Nye. Her Mother, Dorothea "Tena " Mortensen came from Denmark and her Father, Charley "Karl August Karlsson Nöjd" came from Sweden.
