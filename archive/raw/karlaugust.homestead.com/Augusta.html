Augusta

Augusta was the daughter of Gustava Olsdotter and Karl Johan Larsson.  She was the sister of Karl August.  Augusta is the only child, that remained in Sweden, and had descendants.  She had three children. Karl August emigrated to Michigan and had 11 children. 
Augusta was born in Bo Parish and later moved to Orebro.  The above pictures are of the Orebro Castle which is in the center of the city.
Augusta worked at this hotel in Orebro.
Augusta married Emil Thysell and had three children - Oscar, Nils and Svea Maria.
