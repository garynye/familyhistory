Victor
Victor Emanuel Carlsson was born 17 Dec 1872 in Bo Parish, Örebro, Sweden.  He had an organic heart disease.  He died at the age of 41 in Edsberg Parish, Örebro.  Victor was a farm laborer.  He moved to Kvistbro Parish 14 Nov 1902.  He left Kvistbro Parish 27 Oct 1903.  Victor met Sabina Christina Bjork while he was working in Kvistbro Parish.  They had a daughter.  Ellen Kristina Bjork was born 4 May 1904 in Kvistbro Parish, Örebro.
