62. Mikkel OLESEN, son of Oluf Jørgensen Møller and Maren MICHELSDATTER, was born on 12 FEB 1730 in Bierre Sogn, Vejle County, Denmark and died on 19 FEB 1790 in Braae Mølle, Stenderup Parish, Vejle County, Denmark, at age 60.

General Notes: When his daughter, Karen, was born Mikkel Olesen is called Michel Møller of Braae Møller. His death record in Stenderup Parish referrs to him as Michel Olufsen Møller.

I am not certain that I have correctly identified Michel's parents. Michel's birth record states only that he is the Miller's son. Michel is the second son of Oluf Jørgensen's second marriage. Therefore he should be named after his mother's father and of course Michel's father's name was Oluf.

Mikkel married Anne Kirstine RASMUSDATTER on 5 OCT 1775 in Bierre Sogn, Vejle County, Denmark.

Marriage Notes: The marriage record states that widow Michel Olufsen Møller of Broe married Ane Kierstine Rasmusdatter.

I think this because her daughter named her first daughter Karen and the first child born to Michel Olesen and his second wife was named Karen. It was the tradition to name the first daughter after the wife's mother. If there was a subseqent marriage it was traditional to name the first child after the dead spouse.
