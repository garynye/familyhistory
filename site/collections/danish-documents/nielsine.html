Nielsine Mortensen

Nielsine was born 2 March 1876 in Glud Mose, Glud Parish, Vejle county, Denmark. She married Christian Sorensen and had 5 children.

Nielsine Mortensen's birth record

Nielsine's birth record is the last one on the page. The witnesses are the child's parents and young girl Ane Nielsen. Ane Nielsen was Abelone's sister. We have a letter that she wrote to Abelone in 1891.
