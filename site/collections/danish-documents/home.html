Mortensen Family

Transcribed from the original Homestead page.

Hans Jorgen Mortensen and Abelone Nielsen emigrated from Denmark to the USA with 4 children. While in the USA they had 3 more children.

Dorothea "Tena"

Ane Marie "Mary"

Nielsine "Sena"

Morten.

Carl

Farming is such fun!

Gertrude, Hans, Abelone, Carl, Martha, Sena, Tena & Morten
