Morten Jorgen Mortensen

Morten was born 7 December 1878 in Glud Mose, Glud Parish, Vejle County, Denmark. He married Eva Smith and had 3 children.

Eva & Morten Mortensen.

Morten was in the Civiian Conservation Corps fom 1934-1935. He was 55 years old. He was a woodsman and was responsible for reforestration around St Ignace and Ozark, Michigan.
