Ane Kirstine Andersdatter

Anders Jensen & Else Marie Mikkelsdatter had several children. Among them were two sisters, Ane Kirstine & Anne Marie. Anne Marie was Abelone's mother. Ane Kirstine was the ancestor of the Danish cousins that I visited on my trip to Denmark May 2008.

These two pictures are of the house that Ane Kirstine lived in.
